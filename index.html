How to read this Signal (dBm): closer to 0 is stronger. −50 or better is excellent, −60 good, −70 fair, −80 weak.
SNR: signal minus background noise. Above 25 dB is healthy; below 15 dB expect drops.
Link speed: what your Mac and the router negotiated, not real throughput.
Channel width: wider (80/160 MHz) is faster but overlaps more neighbours.
Resolution: channel-level only. Per-subcarrier CSI heatmaps need ESP32-style hardware.
6 GHz networks are not drawn in the waterfall.
